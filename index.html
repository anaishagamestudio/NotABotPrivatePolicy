Privacy Policy for Not A Bot

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Anaisha Game Studio ("we", "our", or "us") developed and publishes Not A Bot ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Not A Bot does not directly ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The App does use Unity Analytics, a service provided by Unity Technologies, to collect analytics information about the use and performance of the App.

Unity Analytics

We use Unity Analytics to understand how players use the App, improve gameplay and performance, and identify technical or usability issues.

Depending on the Unity Analytics configuration and the information available from the device, analytics data may include information such as:

  • A randomly generated installation or player identifier
  • Device and application information
  • Game and gameplay events
  • Application performance information
  • Country or approximate geographic information

We do not intentionally use Unity Analytics to collect precise GPS location information, names, passwords, or other directly identifying information from users.

Unity may process analytics information in accordance with its own privacy practices and policies.

For more information about Unity's privacy practices, please visit: Unity Privacy Policy .

Advertising

Not A Bot does not display advertisements and does not use advertising networks.

In-App Purchases

Not A Bot does not currently offer in-app purchases.

Player Accounts and Login

Not A Bot does not require users to create a separate player account or log in to use the App.

Purchasing or downloading the App through Google Play may require the user to have a Google Play account. This is separate from any account or login system operated by Anaisha Game Studio.

Location Information

Not A Bot does not request or intentionally collect precise device location information such as GPS coordinates.

Unity Analytics may collect country or approximate geographic information as part of its analytics and service operation.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Unity Analytics for analytics and related application services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party service providers.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email address below.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Analytics information may be retained by Unity according to Unity's applicable policies and retention practices. Anaisha Game Studio does not maintain a separate database of users' personal information collected through the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
